Documentary Content Studio

A private, single-owner production tool for the owner's own YouTube documentary channels.

What this is

Documentary Content Studio is an independent studio that researches, writes and produces short and long history, science and nature documentaries for its own YouTube channels. This website describes the small software tool the studio uses to manage those channels. The tool is not offered to the public: it is used only by the studio's owner, on the owner's own computer, for the owner's own channels.

What the tool does with YouTube

With the owner's permission, granted through Google's standard sign-in screen, the tool uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload finished videos to the owner's own channels as private videos, with captions and a playlist;
  • for Shorts only: schedule publication automatically, and only after the owner has given an explicit, video-specific and time-specific approval (one approval covers exactly one Short at one time);
  • read the owner's own channel and video statistics (for example views and audience retention), to evaluate and improve future videos.

Long-form videos always stay under the owner's direct control: the tool only prepares them as private videos; the owner chooses the title and thumbnail and publishes or schedules them personally.

The tool never publishes anything on its own, never comments, never manages other people's channels and never sells data. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
